Why I coach

Hi, I'm Paya.

So many people put time and money into training that isn't getting them anywhere. Random workouts, no plan, nobody checking if it's working. I coach because I want to fix that.

I have a Bachelor of Kinesiology from the University of Toronto and I'm finishing my Master's in Sport Sciences there. I train and race too, from marathons to HYROX, so I try to be my own lab rat as often as I can.

This whole fitness thing is simple, honestly: you should always know what you're doing, why you're doing it, and that it's working. It's my job to explain why you're getting value out of everything we do, not to ask you to blindly follow what I recommend.

Questions

Good to Know.

Do I Need Experience to Start?

No. Some of my clients had never lifted before, others have trained for years. Your plan starts where you are.

How Does Online Coaching Work?

You get a custom program for your goal and equipment, weekly check-ins, feedback on your form videos, and you can message me any time. Your program and progress live in your own app. It starts with a 3-month commitment.

Where Do In-Person Sessions Happen?

1-on-1 and semi-private sessions are in person in Toronto. Sessions are 60 minutes.

How Often Should I Train?

Most clients train with me one to three times a week. We'll decide what makes sense for your goal and schedule on the intro call.

What Happens on the Intro Call?

It's free and there's no pressure. We talk about your goals, your schedule and your training history, and figure out whether online or in person fits you best. Phone, video or in person at the gym, your choice.

What Is the Client App?

It's a private page made just for you. It shows your upcoming sessions, your lifts and personal bests, your goals and your payments. You log in with your own PIN.
