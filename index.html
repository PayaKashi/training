Why I coach

Hi, I'm Paya.

So many people put time and money into training that isn't getting them anywhere. I have a Bachelor of Kinesiology from U of T, I'm finishing my Master's in Sport Sciences there, and I train and race myself, from marathons to HYROX, so I try to be my own lab rat as often as I can.

You should always know what you're doing, why you're doing it, and that it's working. Technique first, progress you can see in your numbers, and habits you can actually keep. It's my job to explain why, not to ask you to follow blindly.

No. Some of my clients had never lifted before, others have trained for years. Your plan starts where you are.

How Often Should I Train?

Most clients train with me one to three times a week. We'll decide what makes sense for your goal and schedule on the intro call.

What Happens on the Intro Call?

It's free and there's no pressure. We talk about your goals, your schedule and your training history, and figure out whether online or in person fits you best. Phone, video or in person, your choice.
