Questions

Good to know.

Do I need experience to start?

No. Some of my clients had never lifted before, others have trained for years. Your plan starts where you are.

Where do sessions happen?

1-on-1 and partner sessions are at HigherGround in Toronto. Online coaching works anywhere.

How often should I train?

Most clients train with me one to three times a week. We'll decide what makes sense for your goal and schedule on the intro call.

How do payments work?

You can pay per session or prepay for a block. Either way, your sessions and payments are all laid out in your app, so there's never any confusion.

What is the client app?

It's a private page made just for you. It shows your upcoming sessions, your lifts and personal bests, your goals and your payments. You log in with your own PIN.
